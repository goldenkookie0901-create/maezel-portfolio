Background & Capabilities

About Maezel L. Obogne

Dedicated Information Officer, writer, and website content manager advancing public service through clear communication and digital technology.

I am a results-oriented Information Officer I and Website Content Manager currently serving at the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) under the Internal Communications Division. I hold a Bachelor of Arts in English Language from Far Eastern University (FEU, Class of 2019).

My professional focus centers on government communications, editorial craftsmanship, and digital platform management. I am experienced in drafting official announcements, advisories, news releases, feature articles, and speeches that translate complex fiscal policies into transparent, citizen-friendly information.

Beyond traditional communications, I actively leverage web technologies—including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Content Management Systems (CMS)—to structure and maintain responsive digital information repositories. I believe that timely, accurate, and accessible public information is fundamental to organizational trust and public service excellence.
